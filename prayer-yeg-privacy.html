Prayer YEG Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Prayer YEG is made by c12n Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

What the app does on your device

  • Prayer times are calculated on your phone for Edmonton, using a fixed location built into the app. The app does not read your device location.
  • Your settings, prayer tracker entries, bookmarks, and downloaded Quran audio are stored on your device only.
  • Prayer alerts use your phone's notification and alarm features so alerts arrive on time, including after a restart.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose it, you can back up your app settings to your own Google Drive, in a private folder only this app can see. This uses Google Sign-In, only when you start a backup, and only your settings are uploaded. c12n Apps never sees this backup. You can delete it from your Google account at any time.

Network requests the app makes

  • The app checks a public file on GitHub about once every 6 hours to see if a newer version is available. No personal information is sent with that check.
  • If you download Quran audio, the audio files come from download.quranicaudio.com.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts are required to use the app. Every feature works without signing in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking tools.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it does not share anything with c12n Apps or anyone else.

Children

The app is a general-audience prayer times app. It does not collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
