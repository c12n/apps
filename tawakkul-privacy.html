Tawakkul Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Tawakkul (Android package com.c12n.Tawakkul) is made by c12n Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

What the app does on your device

  • The Quran text and translations are built into the app, so reading and searching work fully offline.
  • Your settings, playlists, bookmarks, Khatmah progress, and downloaded audio are stored on your device only.

Network requests the app makes

  • Recitation audio is streamed, and downloaded if you choose, from public audio CDNs (the Islamic Network CDN and mp3quran). No personal information is sent with those requests.
  • Tafsir and word-by-word data are fetched from the Quran.com API the first time you open them. No personal information is sent with that request.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts are required to use the app. Every feature works without signing in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking tools.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it does not share anything with c12n Apps or anyone else.

Children

The app is a general-audience Quran app. It does not collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
